Recovery is part of having a baby

In many cultures, the weeks after birth are protected time for the mother. Most families today get a few days in the hospital and then go home to broken sleep and very little help.

Nurture & Rest fills that gap. You stay in a private wing of a boutique hotel, with a secure nursery nearby, and a small team that handles the rest. We take no more than five families a month, so the space stays calm.

What’s included in your care

Overnight newborn care

Experienced nursery staff care for your baby through the night, so you get uninterrupted sleep. Your baby comes to you for feeds and cuddles.

Nourishing meals

Three warm meals a day plus broths and teas, made with the hotel’s chefs to support milk supply and healing.

Lactation and body care

On-site sessions with an IBCLC lactation consultant, gentle pelvic floor guidance, and in-suite restorative massage.
